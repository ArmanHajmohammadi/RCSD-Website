    Governing equations

    Course notation (RCSD notes, Ch. 8, PDF pp. 67–70 and 79–80). Depths di are measured from the extreme compression fibre; steel strain and stress are positive in compression.

    • Moments are taken about mid-depth h/2. Reinforcement here is always symmetric, so mid-depth is also the plastic centroid.
    • Steel is elastic–perfectly plastic. Bars inside the stress block (di < a) deduct the displaced concrete, 0.85f'c.
    • εy = fy/Es (the notes sometimes round it to 0.002). The tension-controlled limit is εt = 0.005, as in the notes.
    • The spiral option changes only φ and the axial cap. It assumes a code-compliant spiral (ρs ≥ 0.45(Ag/Ach − 1)f'c/fyt), which is unusual for rectangular sections.
    • Short-column capacity only: no slenderness effects, no biaxial bending. Use for learning; check designs by hand.
